About Me

I'm Ibtasam Ahmad, an AI/ML Engineer based in Lahore, Pakistan with 4+ years of experience designing and shipping production-grade Generative AI systems: RAG pipelines, multi-agent AI architectures, LLM fine-tuning, chatbots, voicebots and full-stack AI SaaS products.

My background is in Computational Physics, so linear algebra, probability and optimisation are everyday tools rather than something I outsource to a framework. I fine-tune open-source models such as LLaMA with Unsloth, build hybrid SQL and vector retrieval with FAISS, Pinecone and Weaviate, and orchestrate AI agents with LangGraph, LangChain, AutoGen, CrewAI and the Model Context Protocol (MCP).

I've delivered 25+ AI systems in production for 100+ clients across healthcare, insurance, real estate, fintech, education and security. These systems typically cut manual effort by 60–70%. I'm also a published researcher (LSTM vs. Quantum LSTM on arXiv), an AI writer on Medium, and I share AI insights with 22K+ followers on LinkedIn. I'm open to remote AI engineering roles, freelance projects, relocation, and funded Master's research positions in Generative AI.
